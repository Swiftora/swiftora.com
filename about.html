Our story

Why we're building this

Most listing tools are built around new retail products — fixed SKUs, standardized descriptions, predictable pricing. Secondhand is completely different. Every item has a unique history. Condition, maker marks, era, and small details that buyers care deeply about all change the equation. Generic AI tools don't know the difference between a valuable Fenton vase and a common dime-store piece.

Swiftora is being built by sellers, for sellers. We're building a tool that understands the vocabulary of vintage and resale — one that can identify an item, pull real sold comps, and write a listing that actually converts. Not a chatbot you have to wrestle with, but a focused workflow that gets you from photos to posting in minutes.

The goal is simple: give independent sellers the kind of pricing confidence and listing speed that previously only professional resellers with years of experience could access.
