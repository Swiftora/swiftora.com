What Swiftora is

Swiftora is a new AI tool that helps everyday sellers identify items, draft stronger listings, and make better pricing calls—without spreadsheets or research rabbit holes. Upload photos, answer a few quick prompts, and get a clean draft you can post anywhere.

Listing drafts in seconds

Titles, descriptions, tags, and condition cues—ready to copy/paste.

Pricing confidence

Suggested ranges with clear rationale and confidence signals.

ROI visibility

Track cost vs. sale outcomes to learn what is worth your time.
