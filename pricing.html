Why this pricing

Built for sellers, not investors

We looked at what reseller tools charge. List Perfectly starts at $29/month. Vendoo is $8.99 and up. We're pricing Swiftora to be accessible at every level — and we'll reward early members with locked-in rates.

No free tier by design

We're building a serious tool and charging fairly for it. No free plan means no feature gating and no bait-and-switch pricing later.

Caps protect your cost basis

We enforce generation limits to keep costs predictable for you and us. No surprise bills, no runaway AI costs.
